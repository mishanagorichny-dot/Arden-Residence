02 Questions

Good to know.

Is the booking deposit refundable?

Yes. If you decide not to proceed before signing contracts, the €5,000 booking deposit is returned in full.

Can I use Help to Buy at Arden?

Help to Buy is for first-time buyers purchasing a new home to live in. Homes at Arden are new builds, so you may qualify, subject to Revenue's conditions. We'll share a guide when you register.

What are the annual management fees?

Fees cover the shared spaces, woodland care, insurance of common areas and the on-site team. Final figures are published with Phase One pricing.

Is parking included?

Townhouses include two driveway spaces with EV charging. Apartment and duplex parking is available to buy with selected homes.

When will Phase Two be released?

Phase Two, including The Ash townhouses, is planned for release in 2028. Registered buyers hear first.
